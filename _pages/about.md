Ziye Guo is a research assistant in the thrust of Artificial Intelligence at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Before joining HKUST(GZ), he received his M.S. in Computer & Systems Engineering from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in June 2021 and his B.E. in Internet of Things Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in June 2019.

His research was focused on federated learning, and now turned to Large Language Model(LLM).
